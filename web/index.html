BSFChat ID

One account for BSFChat

BSFChat ID is the account you sign in to BSFChat with. The BSFChat apps send you here to sign in, and every chat server that trusts this BSFChat ID knows you by the same name.

Here you can manage your profile, password, two-factor authentication and the devices and apps signed in to your account.
