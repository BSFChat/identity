Delete account

Permanently deletes your BSFChat ID: your email address, password, two-factor settings, server list, and every session and app sign-in. Your username stays reserved so nobody else can take it. This cannot be undone.

Chat servers keep their own accounts and messages. Deleting your BSFChat ID does not remove what you posted on any chat server; deactivate your account on each server first if you want that gone. See the privacy policy.

Change password

You'll need your current password to set a new one. At least 8 characters. Every other session and app is signed out when it changes.
